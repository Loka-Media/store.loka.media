## 1. What is Loka Media? (Executive Summary)

**Loka Media** is a modern **Print-on-Demand (POD) E-Commerce Platform**. 

It allows artists, influencers, designers, and brands to create, launch, and sell custom merchandise (like T-shirts, hoodies, mugs, and caps) **without spending any money upfront on inventory or shipping**.

### The Whole Process in 3 Simple Steps:
```
┌─────────────────────────┐       ┌─────────────────────────┐       ┌─────────────────────────┐
│  1. Creator Designs &   │  ───► │  2. Customer Buys Item  │  ───► │  3. Item Ships &        │
│     Sets Selling Price  │       │     on Loka Marketplace │       │     Creator Gets Paid!  │
└─────────────────────────┘       └─────────────────────────┘       └─────────────────────────┘
```

## 3. The Creator Journey in 8 Simple Steps

### Step 1: Simple Creator Dashboard
When creators log in, they land on their personal **Creator Hub**. They can see:
* Total products published
* Total store sales
* Current wallet balance ready for bank withdrawal
* Quick single-click buttons to start designing or check earnings

---

### Step 2: Choose Blank Products from 2,300+ Catalog
Creators browse an extensive catalog of over **2,300 premium blank items**:
* **Apparel**: Oversized T-Shirts, Heavyweight Hoodies, Sweatshirts, Tank Tops
* **Lifestyle & Accessories**: Tote Bags, Phone Cases, Mugs, Posters, Hats
* **Easy Filtering**: Filter by Men, Women, Unisex, Kids, or Popular Trending items.

---

### Step 3: Easy 2D Design Studio & Realistic 3D Preview
* **Upload Artwork**: Drag and drop logos, art files, or photos directly onto the product.
* **Add Text**: Type custom text, pick fonts, change colors, and curve wording.
* **Interactive 3D View**: Switch to 3D mode to rotate the product $360^\circ$ and check how artwork looks on different shirt colors (Black, White, Navy, Red, Gray).

---

### Step 4: Set Selling Price & See Instant Net Profit
Before publishing, creators set their retail price. The system shows an **instant transparent cost breakdown**:
* Product Printing & Base Cost
* Platform Processing Fees
* **Exact Creator Net Profit** (What the creator keeps per sale!)

---

### Step 5: One-Click Storefront Publishing
* Creators enter a Product Name (e.g., *"Cyberpunk Vintage Hoodie"*) and Description.
* Click **"Publish Product"**.
* The item immediately goes live on the marketplace with a shareable URL to post on Instagram, YouTube, or TikTok.

---

### Step 6: Automated Printing & Zero Inventory Risk
When a fan buys the product:
1. Customer pays on the store.
2. The order automatically sends to our print facility.
3. The item is printed, quality-checked, and shipped straight to the customer's doorstep.
4. **The creator does zero physical work!**

---

### Step 7: Track Real-Time Sales & Wallet Earnings
In the **Creator Wallet**, creators can track:
* **Available Balance**: Money ready to withdraw immediately.
* **Pending Balance**: Earnings held while recent orders are being printed and shipped.
* **Lifetime Sales**: Total money earned since joining.
* **Transparent Ledger**: Line-by-line breakdown showing exact profit earned for every single item sold.

---

### Step 8: Withdraw Money Direct to International Bank Account
Creators enter their bank account details once and request payouts whenever they want:
* Money is transferred directly into their local bank account via ACH, wire, or direct deposit.

## 5. Real-World Example: Profit Math Made Simple

Here is how simple the money math is for a creator selling a custom hoodie:

```
                  Customer Pays for Hoodie: $45.00
                                  │
      ┌───────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────┐
      ▼                           ▼                           ▼
 Wholesale Product         Platform & Payment          CREATOR NET PROFIT
   & Printing Cost                Handling               (Transferred to Bank)
      $25.00                        $3.50                       $16.50
```

* **Customer Price**: $\$45.00$
* **Printing & Blank Shirt Cost**: $\$25.00$
* **Platform & Processing Fee**: $\$3.50$
* **Creator Keeps Clean Profit**: **$\$16.50$ per shirt sold!**

If the creator sells 100 hoodies, they earn **$\$1,650.00$ net profit** without spending a single dollar upfront!

## 6. Multi-Country Direct Bank Support

Creators around the globe can receive their money directly into their local bank accounts:

| Country / Region | Required Bank Information (Simple Input) |
|---|---|
| 🇺🇸 **United States** | 9-Digit Bank Routing Number + Account Number |
| 🇮🇳 **India** | 11-Character IFSC Code + Account Number |
| 🇬🇧 **United Kingdom** | 6-Digit Sort Code + Account Number |
| 🇪🇺 **European Union** | International IBAN + SWIFT Code |
| 🇨🇦 **Canada** | Transit Number + Institution Number + Account Number |
| 🌐 **Global / Other** | Stripe Connect Direct Card / Account Transfer |
